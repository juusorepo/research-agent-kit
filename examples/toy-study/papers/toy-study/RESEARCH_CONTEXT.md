# Project overview

## Study

- Working title: Toy country wellbeing notes
- Lead researcher: Alex
- Core question: Do country-level mean wellbeing scores differ in this tiny teaching file?

## Data

Document what data are available and their limits. This section is **description**. It does not override the analysis plan, accepted research decision notes, or approved result files.

- Data-use rules are in `policies/data-policy.md`
- Source: fake teaching survey
- Access constraints: individual-level real data are not in this folder; see data-use rules
- Unit of analysis: person nested in country (synthetic). Reports use country aggregates
- Coverage: one cross-section
- Construction path: synthetic development file used as processed teaching data; no raw originals in this folder
- Measurement limitations: teaching labels, not a real instrument
- Known sample risks: tiny file; three country labels
- What the files are: one synthetic person-level file for development only

## Outcomes and predictors

- `wellbeing` — 0–10, higher is more wellbeing (synthetic codebook)
- `country` — three teaching labels (North, East, West)

Nothing else is in scope for this toy.
